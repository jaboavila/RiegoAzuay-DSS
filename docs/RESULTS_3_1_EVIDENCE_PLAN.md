7. simple versus technical information architecture;
8. provenance classes and progressive replacement of reference/modelled values;
9. E0–E4 boundaries, with current evaluation at E2;
10. exported daily plan, scenario files, bilingual report, RDS, and reproducibility bundle;
11. final static/scientific/integration counts and author interface-audit results;
12. limitations: no infrastructure control, rights modification, E4 aggregation, farmer-usability trial, or prospective field validation.
